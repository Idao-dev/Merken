# Logo assets

The Simple Icons SVGs in this directory are local copies of brand icons from the
Simple Icons project, used as visual references for the supported software.

- Source: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons
- License: CC0-1.0 for the Simple Icons project, subject to the trademarks and
  brand guidelines of each respective owner.
- `photos.svg` and `media-player.svg` are small local fallback illustrations for
  software families without a matching Simple Icons slug.

Merken is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the cited software
publishers.
